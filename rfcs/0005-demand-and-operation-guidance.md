# RFC 0005 — Observed demand and bounded operation guidance

Status: implemented experimental native contracts; observational audit; no live
consumer, learned selector, or demonstrated burden reduction.

## Pressure and ownership

RFC 0004 established a model-free residual gate and exact, proportional numerical
execution. The remaining limitation is empirical: identifying decisions worth
improving. Ordinary activity reveals demand, but current observations do not
establish which alternative would have preserved outcomes or saved work.

Environment owns environmental observation, ambience, service opportunities,
freshness and orchestration. Control, Language/LSP, Test/Forge, Doctor, Fabric and
RAVEL retain their evidence and operation authorities. Models owns only the
model-facing experimental projections and deterministic baseline in this RFC.
No external repository changes or new monitoring service are required.

## Native contract

`demand.mncs` classifies explicitly reviewed, source-referenced tri-view demand
into proven redundancy, potential avoidability, residual selection, missing
capability/delivery, legitimate reasoning, or insufficient evidence. It validates
event/state/source/time joins and exclusions, not source authenticity, equivalence
or the review's truth. Fewer calls never automatically means better service.

`guidance.mncs` represents a specific unresolved selection among 1–8 independently
addressed, same-contract deterministic candidates. Actual-length bounded views
avoid padding and parameter payload. The exact immutable candidate set is pinned
by an externally authenticated permitted-set reference. Static facts are operation
contracts/identities; dynamic inputs are the decision-state snapshot, availability,
readiness, costs, budgets and external deterministic resolution. There is no
mutable model state, sharing/revision runtime or operation execution in this slice.
Changed observations or connections require a new exact problem/decision artifact
from the existing owner. No private serializer, registry or digest is introduced.

The established operation bypasses ranker/cost/model requirements after validating
its membership and usable source. Residual selection uses unique least declared
cost, with explicit units and provenance. Missing evidence/cost is excluded;
contradictory or misaligned usable evidence refuses; ties abstain. Results retain
source/set/method references and budget counts. They confer no permissions.

Separate SelectionOutcome records admit only observed, joined, post-decision
results for the actually selected operation. Usefulness, work verification and
call-count availability remain independent. Evaluation recomputes the exact
selection and never infers an unchosen outcome or causal savings.

## Verification and research boundary

Twenty-six new native tests extend the preserved 45 tests. Callable probes expose
complete invocation cost at one, two and eight candidates, deterministic bypass,
classification and incomplete-history refusal. No host-language guidance/model
semantics are added. Generic record-carried bounded iteration uses current Source
0.18 facilities; no compiler dependency change was needed.

The audit identifies historical exact-checkout delivery and source-format
projection gaps, with owner-recorded successful deterministic readback. Selected
retained terminal jobs lack sufficient joins/outcomes for a utility cohort.
Existing synthetic benchmark losses, unproven LLM direct-weight efficiency and
the previous real-evidence limitations remain findings, not cleared milestones.

A future learned adapter must reuse the canonical Model and residual/composition
machinery. It requires a legitimate unresolved selection cohort, same-information
deterministic alternative, independent outcomes, disjoint evidence partitions,
bounded revisions and total resource/maintenance accounting. This RFC introduces
no autonomous learning, planning, operation scheduling or operational integration.
